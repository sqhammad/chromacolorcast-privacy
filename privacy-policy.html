Privacy Policy for Chroma Color Sort

Last updated: 9 September 2026
Application: Chroma Color Sort (Android package com.chromacolorsort.game)

Chroma Color Sort ("the Game", "we", "us") is a single-player colour-sorting puzzle game. This policy explains what information is handled when you play the Game, why it is handled, and the choices you have. We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, or give us your name, email address, or phone number.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. We operate no servers, accounts, or analytics of our own, and we receive no personal information from you directly.

Your game progress — current level, best scores, coin balance, and sound, music, and haptics preferences — is saved only in the Game's private storage on your own device. It is never transmitted to us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data from Android Settings.

2. Information collected by Google AdMob

The Game displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which is how the Game is funded. The SDK collects and shares the following categories of information automatically for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention:

InformationHow it is used
IP address Delivering ads and estimating the approximate (city-level or broader) location of the device.
Advertising ID, app set ID, and other device or account identifiers Selecting and measuring ads, capping how often an ad is shown, and detecting invalid traffic.
Interactions with ads and the Game (app opens, taps, video views) Measuring ad performance and rewarding you correctly when you choose to watch a rewarded ad.
Diagnostic information (for example load times and errors) Monitoring the reliability and performance of the ads and the SDK.

This information is collected by Google as an independent controller or processor under its own terms; we never receive it in an identifiable form, only aggregate earnings reports. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. All data collected by the SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

3. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. On Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device) to reset your advertising ID or delete it entirely, which stops the Game's ads from being personalised.
  • Opt out of personalised ads. Use the same Android ads settings, or manage Google ad personalisation at My Ad Center. You will still see ads, but they will be non-personalised.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. Where required by law, you are shown a consent message before personalised ads are served, and you may decline or change your choice later.
  • Delete your data. Because everything we hold stays on your device, uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage erases all of it. Requests relating to data held by Google should be directed to Google using the links above.

4. Permissions the Game uses

The Game requests internet access so ads can load. It does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, files, precise location, or any other sensitive permission, and it does not read or write data outside its own private storage.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone other than the advertising provider described in Section 2. We may disclose information if required to do so by law or valid legal process.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be reflected in the Game's Google Play listing.
